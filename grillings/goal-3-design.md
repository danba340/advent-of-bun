# Goal 3 - Design

Five HTML variants for Lisa to pick. Approved text from `portfolio/text.md`. Screenshot files are named placeholders until she replaces them.

## Plans (reviewed against generic defaults)

Avoided: cream + terracotta serif, black + acid accent, newspaper hairlines, SaaS cards, tracked all-caps eyebrows, middle-dot meta, monospace data chips.

1. **Duty roster** — hospital shift board. Barlow condensed + Source Serif 4. Board grey, ink green-black, one tape-yellow mark on the name. Projects as roster rows, not a sequence of 01–03.
2. **Corridor** — porter in a hospital corridor. Figtree. Steel and linoleum. One acute-red moment on the acute-transport sentence. Porter stills in a phone-width column.
3. **Work order** — printed transport order. IBM Plex Sans. Blue copy-paper head band. Contact as form lines, sentence case.
4. **Ward notes** — SASICU observation notes. Literata only. Ballpoint blue, notebook gutter. Quiet research document.
5. **Birch shop** — outdoor workshop without Husqvarna orange. Archivo. Birch, walnut, aluminum bars between projects.
